# Standard Chartered Credit Analyst Virtual Experience – Green Solutions Case Study

**Completed as part of the official Standard Chartered Bank Credit Analyst Virtual Experience Program (Forage)**

This portfolio contains the full deliverables from the simulation, organised exactly as per the program structure.

---

## Task 1: Financial Statement Analysis & Risk Assessment

**What I learned**
- How to analyse financial statements to assess a client’s financial stability
- Best practices for conducting risk assessment using key financial ratios

**What I did**
- Assessed the full financial statements of Green Solutions Manufacturing Ltd (FY2022–FY2023)
- Calculated and interpreted key ratios (profitability, liquidity, leverage, interest coverage)
- Produced a preliminary report highlighting the client’s financial position and potential risks

**Deliverables in this folder:**
- `Original_Financial_Statements.xlsx` – Raw data provided in the simulation
- `Analyzed_Ratios_Report.xlsx` – Full ratio calculations with commentary
- `Preliminary_Report_Green_Solutions.docx` – Short preliminary risk assessment report
- `Financial_Health_Analysis_Report.docx` – Detailed narrative report with findings and risks

---

## Task 2: Industry Analysis & Video Presentation

**What I learned**
- Methods for conducting industry analysis and understanding macroeconomic factors
- Effective ways to prepare and present industry research

**What I did**
- Performed industry research on the reusable consumer products sector
- Compiled findings into a clear video presentation summarising Task 1 + Task 2 results and implications for Green Solutions’ creditworthiness

**Deliverables in this folder:**
- `Industry_Reports/` – Public market reports on reusable coffee cups and water bottles (used for context)
- `Video_Recording_Script_10sec.docx` – Concise 10-second summary script (ready to record)
- `Video_Recording_Script_2min_Max.docx` – Full 2-minute video script version
- `Video_Recording_Script_Green_Solutions.docx` – Extended narrative version

---

## Key Skills Demonstrated

- Financial statement analysis & ratio interpretation
- Credit risk assessment using quantitative and qualitative factors
- Industry & macroeconomic context analysis
- Clear written and verbal communication of credit recommendations
